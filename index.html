Operational Guidelines

1. Subscription & Account Activation

Users must subscribe to a plan (Starter, Regular, or Family) to unlock automated pickup scheduling and real-time collector tracking[cite: 1].

2. "I'm Not Home" Protocol

Customers tag their pickup with the "I'm Not Home" checkbox, specifying where the secure waste bag is left outside. Collectors verify via photo upon pickup[cite: 1].

3. Surcharges & Additional Fees

Additional collection fees are automatically calculated and charged depending on the customer's exact location and distance zone[cite: 1].

4. Recycling & Partner Disposal

Sorted recyclables are segregated and delivered to certified local recycling partner centers, ensuring full environmental compliance[cite: 1].
